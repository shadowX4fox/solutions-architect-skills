# BIAN Service Landscape v14.0.0 — the open corpus

The banking capability model this skill maps a Product Owner Specification onto. **The taxonomy as BIAN publishes it,
unadapted** — nothing renamed, merged, added or dropped — with only what may be redistributed.

**Release 14.0.0.** Counts: **8 Business Areas · 43 Business Domains · 341 Service Domains**, of which 258 have a
published semantic API and 83 do not.

**What this copy carries, and what it does not.** BIAN publishes its semantic APIs under the **Apache License 2.0**
(<https://github.com/bian-official/public>, `LICENSE-BIAN-APIS` here); the Service Landscape's role definitions and
examples of use on bian.org are **© BIAN, all rights reserved**. So each Service Domain keeps its path, BIAN id and API,
and its **What it does.** is its API's own one-line `info.description` (257 of them; 84 Service Domains have no API, or an
API published with an empty description, and carry none). **No example of use is carried.** An organization's
**institution pack** (`docs/INSTITUTION_PACKS.md`) supplies the full landscape text, which the skill then prefers
(`[bian_dir]`); a Service Domain with no description here is never judged, and the report says so.

Generated by `scripts/build-open-landscape.js <full landscape> <bian-official/public checkout> --out <dir>` — never hand-edited.

## The hierarchy

BIAN organises banking capability as a three-level chain. Each level maps onto something this skill emits:

| BIAN level | Count | What this skill does with it |
|---|---|---|
| **Business Area** | 8 | The top-level classification unit of the alignment report. Carries the `DOM` segment of a Diagram ID. |
| **Business Domain** | 43 | The unit that carries the `CAP` segment of a Diagram ID, and the diagram folder it names. |
| **Service Domain** | 341 | The atomic unit — a single, non-overlapping banking capability such as `Current Account` or `Card Collections`. Reported as evidence and carried as a **tag**, never as an ID segment. |

The Service-Domain rule is the standard's, not this skill's: *"Below the Business Domain, BIAN defines 341 Service Domains; the Service Domain is a tag and a register column, never an ID segment"* (`llms-rules.txt` §1.3).

## Load contract

**`llms-bian.txt` is the default and usually the only file you need.** At 431 lines it carries every name, id and API link in the landscape — enough to classify a Business Area, resolve a Business Domain, and list Service Domains by name.

Open an area file **only** when you need a Service Domain's role definition or example of use to justify a classification, and then open **one at a time, only for Business Areas already classified 🟢 or 🟡**.

**Never read all eight.** They total 4,693 lines (~120K tokens) and would blow the main-session working set — the same rule the design language applies to `llms-full.txt`.

| You need… | Read |
|---|---|
| which Business Area / Business Domain a named Service Domain sits in | `llms-bian.txt` § Service Domains |
| the complete Service Domain list for a Business Domain | `llms-bian.txt` § Business Domains |
| whether a Service Domain has a published API, and its spec URL | `llms-bian.txt` § Service Domains, or § Service Domains without a published API |
| a Service Domain's role definition or example of use | the one area file for its Business Area |
| a `DOM` or `CAP` code | **not here** — `skills/architecture-docs/references/design-language/bian-registry.json` (optional: ships with the Design Language Standard) |

## Codes are not in this folder

These files carry BIAN's own vocabulary and ids. They do **not** carry the `DOM` and `CAP` shortcodes — those are the SA Design Language Standard's only addition to BIAN, and they live in that standard's `bian-registry.json`, which comes with an institution pack.

That separation is deliberate and load-bearing. The BIAN corpus is a published industry model that any bank can adopt; the shortcodes are one organization's. A project without a design language in force still gets a complete alignment report from these files — it just does not get the **Diagram ID Segments** section. Never mint a `DOM` or `CAP` value from a name in this folder.

## Updating

1. From the full landscape (an institution pack's `bian/`) and a checkout of `bian-official/public` at the new release:
   `bun scripts/build-open-landscape.js <full> <checkout> --out references/bian-v14`. Do not merge or patch by hand.
2. Keep the hyphenated filenames — `llms-bian.txt` links to them.
3. Re-check the counts stated at the top of this file and in `SKILL.md`, `ANALYSIS_METHODOLOGY.md` and `BIAN_ALIGNMENT_TEMPLATE.md`.
4. When a design language is in force, confirm every `CAP` in its `bian-registry.json` still resolves to a Business Domain present here.
