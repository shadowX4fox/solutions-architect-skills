# BIAN Service Landscape v14.0.0

> Complete index of the 341 BIAN Service Domains in release 14.0.0, arranged under 43 Business Domains and 8 Business Areas. This file is the index: use it to answer questions that need a *complete* answer — every Service Domain in a Business Area or Business Domain, which Service Domains have a published API, or where a named Service Domain sits in the hierarchy. Descriptions, examples of use, and API detail live in the eight per-area files linked below.

BIAN (Banking Industry Architecture Network) organises banking capability as a three-level chain: Business Area contains Business Domains, which contain Service Domains. A Service Domain is the atomic unit — a single, non-overlapping banking capability such as Current Account or Card Collections.

Counts for release 14.0.0: 8 Business Areas, 43 Business Domains, 341 Service Domains, of which 258 have a published semantic API and 83 do not.

Entry format in the Service Domains section below: `[Service Domain](file#anchor): Business Area > Business Domain · BIAN id · [API file](specification URL)`. The specification URL is the authoritative link to that Service Domain's published OpenAPI 3 specification on GitHub; always return it alongside the file name. The AsyncAPI 3 specification of the same name sits under `release14.0.0/semantic-apis/asyncapi-3.x/yamls/`.

Every name and identifier is as published by BIAN — the taxonomy only. Descriptions in the area files are the semantic APIs' own (Apache License 2.0); the Service Landscape's role definitions are © BIAN and come with an institution pack.
