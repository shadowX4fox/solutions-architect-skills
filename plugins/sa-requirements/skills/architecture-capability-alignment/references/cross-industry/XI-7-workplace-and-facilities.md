This document covers **one** of the 9 cross-industry Domains: **Workplace & Facilities** (id `MC-70`). It contains 2 Capability Groups and 9 Capabilities.

The 9 Domains are: Enterprise Governance & Risk; Corporate Finance; Human Capital; Customer & Commercial; Supply Chain & Procurement; Technology & Information; Workplace & Facilities; Quality, Innovation & Knowledge; Project, Change & Process.
