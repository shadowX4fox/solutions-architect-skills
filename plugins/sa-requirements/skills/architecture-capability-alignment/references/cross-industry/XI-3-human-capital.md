# Cross-Industry Capability Map — Domain: Human Capital

This document covers **one** of the 9 cross-industry Domains: **Human Capital** (id `MC-30`). It contains 1 Capability Group and 9 Capabilities.

Acquiring, developing, deploying, rewarding, and supporting the workforce so the enterprise has the right people, in the right roles, performing at the right level.

The 9 Domains are: Enterprise Governance & Risk; Corporate Finance; Human Capital; Customer & Commercial; Supply Chain & Procurement; Technology & Information; Workplace & Facilities; Quality, Innovation & Knowledge; Project, Change & Process. Capabilities belonging to the other 8 Domains are documented in their own files.

Each Capability below states its path, catalogue id, **What it does.** (the catalogue's own description) and, where the catalogue decomposes it further, the names of the capabilities it **Includes.**
